## Part 2: Website

A single-page, scroll-based experience. One continuous scroll, each section full viewport height, smooth animations.

**Tech:** Plain HTML, CSS, vanilla JS. GSAP + ScrollTrigger for scroll animations (loaded via CDN). Responsive design (mobile-first). Soft background music toggle (muted by default, since browsers block autoplay-with-sound). No login, no backend, purely static so it can be hosted on GitHub Pages free forever.

**Visual style:** Warm ivory & charcoal base, burgundy accent, dusty blush & soft gold highlights. Large elegant serif for headlines, clean sans-serif for body, script/handwriting font for personal accents. Floating sparkle particles, parallax effects, smooth fade/slide transitions. Ken Burns zoom on hero photos. No confetti, no balloons, no clip-art. Polished, cinematic, romantic.

**Site sections (top to bottom):**

### 1. Loading Screen
Elegant preloader: her initial "A" or a thin animated line drawing itself, fading into the hero as images load.

### 2. Hero Section
- Full-screen background photo: a strong couple photo (e.g., `couple01.jpg` — walking at night with warm lights) with a soft dark gradient overlay.
- Ken Burns zoom effect (slow, gentle).
- Her name "AANYA" in huge elegant serif, animates in letter by letter.
- Subline beneath, fading in after: "23 years. Still my favourite person."
- Soft pulsing "scroll to begin" prompt at the bottom.

### 3. Opening Message
Warm ivory background. Message reveals one line at a time as the user scrolls, each fading and sliding up gently:
```
Hi love.
I built this just for you.
Not because you need reminding —
but because I wanted the world to know,
even for one day,
how much you mean to me.
```

### 4. Love Language Section
Full-screen section titled "A few things I don't say enough" in elegant serif. Beneath it, 5 short lines fade in one at a time on scroll or tap, each with a small heart or sparkle icon:
```
You are the warmest person I know.
Your laugh is my favourite sound.
You make ordinary days feel like something worth keeping.
I am endlessly proud of you.
Being loved by you is the best thing that has happened to me.
```

### 5. Photo Gallery / "Pieces of Us"
Horizontal scroll-snap row OR staggered masonry grid using 8–10 of the best couple photos (e.g., `couple01.jpg`, `couple04.jpg`, `couple11.jpg`, `couple19.jpg`, `couple23.jpg`, `couple26.jpg`, `couple29.jpg`, `couple32.jpg`). Each photo fades/scales in as it enters view, with a soft tilt-on-hover effect on desktop. Under each photo, a short caption (you'll fill in later as placeholders). Section heading: "Pieces of us."

### 6. Letter Section
Calm, parchment-style background texture (subtle). Your full personal letter, rendered in clean serif with generous line spacing. Text reveals gradually as the user scrolls down through it (not all at once). Use the exact text from Page 10 of the magazine.

### 7. Interactive Wish Section
Full-screen. Large line: "Make a wish." Beneath it: "The rest of this year is yours to write." A single animated candle flame the visitor can click/tap to "blow out," triggering a soft burst of glowing particles and a gentle fade to the next section. Purely visual, no sound required.

### 8. Closing / Signature Section
Final full-screen. A beautiful couple photo in the background with a dark overlay. Large text: "Happy Birthday, Aanya." Smaller text in handwriting font beneath: "I love you. Today, tomorrow, and every ordinary day after that." A small looping animated heart or sparkle beneath. Tiny signature line at the very bottom: "— Anant".

### Technical Requirements

- Preload all images; show the elegant loading screen rather than a blank flash.
- Lazy-load gallery images below the fold for performance.
- **All editable text in one spot:** Put every headline, line, caption, and message in one clearly marked config object at the top of the JavaScript file, so wording can be changed later without touching the rest of the code.
- Add Open Graph meta tags (title "Happy Birthday, Aanya", description, and a strong couple photo as the preview image) so the link looks good when shared.
- Compress/resize images for web without losing visible quality.
- No analytics, no tracking scripts, no ads.
- Include a `README.md` explaining how to run locally and how to deploy to GitHub Pages.
