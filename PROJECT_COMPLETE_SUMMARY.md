## 🎬 THE 8-SECTION WEBSITE STRUCTURE

### **Section 1: Loading Screen**
- Elegant preload with her initial "A" drawing itself
- Fades into hero as images load
- ~2–3 seconds

### **Section 2: Hero Section**
- Full-bleed background photo (couple01.jpg) with Ken Burns zoom
- Name "AANYA" animates in letter-by-letter (elegant serif)
- Subline: "23 years. Still my favourite person."
- Soft pulsing "scroll to begin" prompt

### **Section 3: Opening Message**
- Warm ivory background
- Message reveals line-by-line on scroll:
  ```
  Hi love.
  I built this just for you.
  Not because you need reminding —
  but because I wanted the world to know,
  even for one day,
  how much you mean to me.
  ```

### **Section 4: Love Language Section**
- Title: "A few things I don't say enough"
- 5 love lines fade in one-by-one:
  - You are the warmest person I know.
  - Your laugh is my favourite sound.
  - You make ordinary days feel like something worth keeping.
  - I am endlessly proud of you.
  - Being loved by you is the best thing that has happened to me.

### **Section 5: Photo Gallery — "Pieces of Us"**
- 8–10 couple photos in gallery or horizontal scroll
- Suggested photos: couple02, couple04, couple07, couple10, couple13, couple16, couple19, couple22
- Each fades in and scales as it enters viewport
- Soft tilt-on-hover (desktop)

### **Section 6: Letter Section**
- Parchment texture background
- Full personal letter (from magazine Page 10)
- Text reveals gradually as user scrolls
- Signature: "With love, Anant Agrawal"

### **Section 7: Interactive Wish Section**
- Background photo (couple25.jpg) with dark overlay
- Centered text: "Make a wish. The rest of this year is yours to write."
- Animated candle flame in the center
- On click: Flame grows, sparkles burst outward, soft "poof" sound plays
- Auto-fades to next section

### **Section 8: Closing / Signature Section**
- Background photo (couple28.jpg) with dark overlay
- Large text: "Happy Birthday, Aanya."
- Subtext: "I love you. Today, tomorrow, and every ordinary day after that."
- Signature: "— Anant"
- Looping heart pulse beneath
