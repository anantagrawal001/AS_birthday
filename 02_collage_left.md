## Shared style block (paste at the start of every prompt)

Create one flat, straight-on portrait magazine page. A4 proportions (210:297). Edge-to-edge artwork at the highest native resolution. No mockup, no book, no perspective, no hands, no curled paper, no surrounding scenery.

Look: fashion editorial x retro scrapbook x personal photo diary. Stylish and nostalgic, not childish or cluttered.

Palette: warm ivory, deep charcoal, burgundy, dusty blush. Muted sage only for small flower accents.

Typography: large elegant fashion-editorial serif headlines. Clean, readable sans-serif body text. Restrained handwritten accent only for short captions or signatures. No other fonts.

Decoration (use only what this page calls for): small hand-drawn stars and sparkles, instant-photo frames with broad lower borders, subtle paper grain, torn-paper edges, translucent tape corners, pressed-flower illustrations, retro TV or music-player elements, vintage desktop-window graphics.

Avoid: cartoon clip art, balloons, cakes, confetti, glitter, neon gradients, random font changes, heavy texture over faces, overlapping paragraphs, tiny text, real brand logos, watermarks, shopping overlays, prices, barcodes, QR codes, fake scannable codes, page numbers not listed for this page, and any printed photo labels such as "Photo 02".

Photo rules: use only the attached photos for this page. Preserve each person's exact face, expression, skin tone, hairstyle, clothing and body proportions. Do not beautify, reshape, replace or invent anyone. Do not stretch photos. Crop conservatively, or place the original inside a paper-coloured mat. If a photo cannot be placed without altering the person, leave its frame empty.

Text rules: render all text exactly as written below, with correct spelling and no added words. Keep text and faces inside the page edges, with extra room at the inner binding edge. Do not split a headline or a face across the page.
